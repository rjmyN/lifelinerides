Medical Appointments

Whether it's a routine check-up with your primary care doctor, a specialist consultation, or an outpatient procedure, Lifeline will get you there and back without stress. We plan around your appointment time, arriving early enough so you're never rushed.

Dialysis Transportation

Dialysis patients typically require transportation three times a week, every week. Consistency and reliability are absolutely critical. Lifeline provides dependable recurring rides for dialysis clients, building a schedule you can set your clock to. Missing a dialysis session has serious health consequences — we take that responsibility seriously.
