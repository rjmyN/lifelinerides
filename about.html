About Lifeline Ride Services

We exist because everyone in Louisville deserves a safe, dependable, professional ride — for any destination, at any time.

Our Story

Born from a Real Need in Louisville

Lifeline Ride Services was founded with a simple but powerful belief: every person in Louisville deserves reliable, dignified transportation to their medical appointments — regardless of whether they have a car, a family member available, or the physical ability to drive themselves.

We saw too many people missing critical dialysis sessions, skipping follow-up appointments, and delaying care — not because they didn't want help, but because they couldn't get there. We built Lifeline to change that.

Today, we serve ambulatory clients across the Louisville metro area with a commitment that goes beyond just driving: we show up on time, we treat every passenger with warmth and respect, and we take the stress of transportation completely off your shoulders.
